AI Boom: The 2026 Semiconductor Outlook

Global sales are surging, and South Korea is setting semiconductor export records. Here is what the numbers mean—and what could happen next.

Creator: Ayush KcMaterial verified: October 14, 2026Focus: Global market and South Korea

The big picture: Artificial intelligence (AI) needs powerful processors and fast memory. That demand is helping the semiconductor industry grow. South Korea benefits because Samsung Electronics and SK hynix are major memory-chip suppliers.

“Reported” means recorded results. “Projection” means an estimate that may change.

Five Key Facts

1
Projection · May 2026

Global revenue was expected to reach $1.51 trillion

WSTS projected 90% growth in global semiconductor revenue for 2026. It expected memory revenue to exceed $800 billion.

Spring forecast, not a final result.

2
Reported · First half 2026

Global sales reached $702 billion

Sales rose 102% compared with the first half of 2025. Memory sales rose 305%. WSTS later estimated the full-year market at $1.655 trillion.

The $1.655 trillion figure is a projection.

3
Reported · September 2026

Korean semiconductor exports topped $60 billion in one month

South Korea exported $60.3 billion in semiconductors in September, up 262.8% from September 2025. January–September exports totaled $340.3 billion.

4
Projection · 2026

Korean semiconductor exports could grow more than 15%

KITA forecast growth of more than 15% compared with 2025. Rising memory prices and demand for AI-related chips support this outlook.

Final annual export results were not yet available on the verification date.

5
Reported · First half 2026

High-value memory helped drive Korea’s export growth

Korean semiconductor exports reached $192.4 billion in the first half, up 162.6% year over year. KITA pointed to demand for HBM and DDR5 memory.

Four Important Terms

Click the button to show or hide the definitions.

1. AI semiconductors

Chips that help AI systems learn, calculate, and respond. Examples include GPUs and AI accelerators.

2. Memory semiconductors

Chips that store data and supply it to processors. Examples include DRAM, NAND flash, and HBM.

3. HBM (High-Bandwidth Memory)

A type of memory made by stacking memory layers. It transfers large amounts of data quickly and is useful for AI accelerators.

4. Semiconductor exports

Semiconductor products sold by one country to customers in other countries. Export growth can support businesses and the economy.

Industry Outlook: What Comes Next?

The outlook is strong, but growth is not guaranteed to continue at the same speed.

Growth factors

  • More investment in AI data centers.
  • Higher demand for AI processors and accelerators.
  • Growing need for HBM and server memory.
  • Strong demand may support Korean semiconductor exports.

Uncertainties and risks

  • AI companies could slow their spending.
  • Competition from Chinese memory makers may increase.
  • Some customers are developing their own AI chips.
  • Memory prices can rise or fall quickly.

Bottom line: AI is creating a major opportunity for the semiconductor industry and South Korea. Reported sales and exports show strong growth so far in 2026. Full-year totals remain projections until final data are published.
